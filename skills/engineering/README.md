# Engineering skills

The Ark adapts selected engineering workflows to its vault-first context, repo-local specs and plans, and explicit commit/push authority.

## Model-invoked core

These may be selected by the model or explicitly requested by the user. Their descriptions stay loaded because they govern common engineering transitions.

- **[brainstorming](./brainstorming/SKILL.md)** — clarify and approve a design before behavior changes.
- **[writing-plans](./writing-plans/SKILL.md)** — turn approved requirements into a vault-backed, execution-ready plan.
- **[executing-plans](./executing-plans/SKILL.md)** — execute an approved plan with evidence checkpoints.
- **[test-driven-development](./tdd/SKILL.md)** — use a source-informed red-green-refactor loop.
- **[systematic-debugging](./diagnosing-bugs/SKILL.md)** — establish root cause before proposing a fix.
- **[code-review](./code-review/SKILL.md)** — review a diff against requirements and standards.
- **[verification-before-completion](./verification-before-completion/SKILL.md)** — require fresh evidence before completion claims.
